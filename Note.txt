A PERSONAL NOTE ON INTENT AND EXPECTATIONS

I also want to be clear about why these materials exist.

I developed and shared these concepts because I wanted to help. I hoped that there was a chance that some idea, mechanism, observation, design element, or framework among them might eventually prove useful to someone with the expertise and resources to evaluate it properly.

That hope should not be confused with a belief that these concepts are necessarily correct, that they will be adopted, or that I possess special knowledge about their significance.

I do not know whether any particular concept in this repository will work. I do not know whether any government, institution, company, researcher, engineer, or other recipient will find a particular proposal useful. I do not assume that similarities between my work and later real-world developments demonstrate that my work caused or influenced those developments.

I recognize the difference between possibility and evidence.

The fact that I devoted substantial time to developing ambitious, unusual, or highly speculative concepts should likewise not be interpreted, by itself, as evidence that I am suffering from psychosis, delusion, or another psychiatric condition. Creative experimentation, curiosity, humanitarian motivation, and a willingness to explore improbable ideas are not claims that those ideas are established reality.

My position is deliberately modest: I hoped there was a chance that something here might help.

Perhaps an entire concept is useful. Perhaps only one small mechanism buried inside hundreds of pages is useful. Perhaps qualified experts will examine a project and conclude that it cannot work at all. I accept all of those possibilities.

That is one reason I am releasing these materials openly rather than presenting myself as an authority capable of validating them.

I am offering ideas, not asserting certainty.

I ask that the work be judged on its contents and evidence, and that technical, scientific, economic, policy, or engineering claims be independently evaluated by people qualified to do so.

If nothing comes of these materials, that is an acceptable outcome. If something useful can be extracted from them, then making them available was worthwhile.

Susan Stroud
