Goal: A fine, reactive powder that mimics volcanic ash for cement, grout, or ceramic applications.

Recipe (Makes 100 kg)
Material	Mass	Sieve Size	Role

Step-by-Step Processing Protocol
Coarse crushing: If your materials are gravel-sized, crush them to < 5 mm using a jaw crusher or heavy hammer.

Fine milling: Run the crushed material through a ball mill, roller mill, or hammer mill until 100% passes a 325-mesh (45-micron) sieve. This is non-negotiable. Coarser particles will not react pozzolanically.

Blending: Dry-mix the 60 kg silica and 40 kg granite in a mixer for 10 minutes until perfectly homogenous (no streaks).

Heat activation (The Secret Step): Place the blended powder in a steel tray and fire it in a kiln or furnace at 650°C – 750°C for 1 hour. This partially melts the quartz crystal structure into a reactive amorphous phase. Let it cool completely in the oven.

Final grind: After cooling, give it one more quick pass through the mill to break up any sintered clumps.

For high-strength geopolymer: 80% Path B powder + 20% sodium silicate solution (water glass). Cures in 7 days.

Step-by-Step Mixing & Curing Protocol
Phase 1: Dry Premix (Day 1)

In a large mixer or on a clean concrete floor, combine:

70 kg of your pre-made Path A aggregate (already mixed).

25 kg of your heat-treated Path B binder powder.

5 kg of hydrated lime powder.

Dry-mix thoroughly for 5–7 minutes until the color is completely uniform (pale grey).

Phase 2: Wet Activation (Day 1)

Create a hydration tank: In a separate bucket, dissolve 1 kg of brown sugar or molasses into 15 liters of water. (Sugar acts as a set-retarder, giving you 45 minutes of working time instead of 15 minutes.)

Slowly pour the sugar-water into the dry mix while the mixer is running. Add more water gradually until the mixture reaches a "moist crumb" texture—it should clump when squeezed in your fist but not drip water.

Total water: Typically 15–18 liters per 100 kg of dry mix. Do not exceed 20 liters or it will segregate.

Phase 3: Placement & Compaction (Within 45 minutes)

Dump the wet mix into your formwork or trench (minimum 10 cm thick, 2 m wide).

Compact aggressively: Use a plate compactor, a hand tamper, or the back of a heavy shovel. Compact until the surface is hard and smooth, and a faint sheen of water appears on top. This is critical for strength.

Smooth the surface with a steel trowel to close pores.

Phase 4: Curing (Days 1–14)

Cover immediately with plastic sheeting or wet burlap to prevent rapid drying. The geopolymer reaction requires moisture.

Cure for 7 days minimum before foot traffic. 14 days before driving a vehicle over it.

During the first 3 days, mist the surface with water once daily (under the plastic) to keep it damp.
